# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Use welcoming and respectful language.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus discussion on technical and community issues rather than personal attacks.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experiences, data-use constraints, and institutional responsibilities.
- Protect confidential, proprietary, restricted, and sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment; discriminatory or derogatory comments; intimidation; deliberate disruption; publication of private information without permission; and knowingly submitting malicious, infringing, restricted, or unauthorized material.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers identified in `docs/MAINTAINER_ROLES.md` or through the security contact in `SECURITY.md` when the concern involves a security issue. Maintainers will acknowledge reports, assess the situation, and take proportionate action. Actions may include clarification, warning, moderation, removal of content, or restriction of participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, releases, events, and other project-associated communication channels.
